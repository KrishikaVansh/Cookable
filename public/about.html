Why we built it

Less "what's for dinner?" More "I can make that."

We've all stood in front of an open fridge, sure there's nothing to eat, while a few good ingredients sat right there.

Cookable turns that moment around. Tell us what you have and we'll suggest dishes you can actually cook, point out the one or two things you're missing, and help you waste less food along the way.

How it works

Three steps from pantry to plate

1

Add what you have

Type your ingredients one by one: chicken, garlic, rice, whatever's on hand.

2

See what you can cook

We match you with real dishes and show how closely each one fits your kitchen.

3

Fill in the gaps

Every dish lists what you already have and what you'd need to pick up, plus full step-by-step instructions.
